Atlas Control

Kit Contents

The standard kit contains the controller and the cables needed to connect peripherals.

Controller

  • Flight controller in a protective aluminium enclosure, 98 × 62 × 16 mm
  • Vibration-isolation dampers
  • Conformal-coated PCB
  • microSD card fitted

Power module

  • Power module, up to 14S

Cables

2×Power cable
2×JST-GH 1.25 mm, 6-pin (reversible)
1×JST-GH 1.25 mm, 3-pin
4×JST-GH 1.25 mm, 4-pin
2×JST-GH 1.25 mm, 6-pin
1×JST-GH 1.25 mm, 7-pin — FTDI cable for the DEBUG port
1×JST-GH 1.25 mm, 10-pin
1×Servo cable
DEBUG port
The FTDI cable connects to the DEBUG & UART7 connector and gives access to the system console and the SWD interface.
