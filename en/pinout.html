Pinout

Servo rail

Three rows of contacts: signal, servo power and ground. Outputs are labelled M1–M14, with a separate RC IN contact for the receiver.

ContactFunction
RC INRC receiver input, also SERIAL5 (UART8)
M1 – M14Outputs 1–14

Each output has three contacts: S — signal, + — servo power, − — ground. All 14 outputs are implemented identically.

DShot
Channels 1–12 support DShot; channels 13–14 are PWM only, because their timer has no DMA. Output grouping and the shared rate are covered in the Configuration section.
Servo power
The controller does not supply power to the servo rail. To power servos, connect a BEC to any plus/minus pair on outputs M1–M14. Maximum 10 V.
